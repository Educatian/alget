# ALGET Artifact Revision Rating Codebook

Scale: 0-2 for each dimension. Score only visible evidence in the submitted artifact trace. Do not infer intent from polish or writing fluency.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | 0 Missing | 1 Partial | 2 Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `claim_visibility` | No clear artifact claim. | Claim exists but lacks audience, action, or purpose. | Claim names artifact purpose, learner/audience, and intended action. |
| `constraint_named` | No design/course constraint named. | Generic constraint such as "make it clearer." | Specific constraint from theory, rubric, accessibility, policy, data, or context. |
| `evidence_specificity` | No evidence source. | Evidence is vague or unattributed. | Evidence names a section concept, annotation, rubric line, dataset, observation, or transcript segment. |
| `support_boundary` | AI/support use not described. | Support is described but not bounded. | Support move is bounded and tied to what the learner asked or accepted. |
| `accepted_justified` | Accepted suggestion absent. | Accepted suggestion listed without rationale. | Accepted suggestion is connected to evidence and artifact change. |
| `rejected_justified` | No rejected/modified suggestion. | Rejection listed but weakly explained. | Rejection or modification explains risk, overclaim, mismatch, or missing evidence. |
| `revision_visible` | No before/after change visible. | Revision is present but hard to audit. | Revision is concrete and auditable in the artifact. |
| `limitation_acknowledged` | No limitation/transfer boundary. | Limitation is generic. | Limitation names a condition, audience, context, or evidence gap. |

## Rater Procedure

1. Rate independently before discussion.
2. Use raw learner text only inside the secure research environment.
3. Export only scores, target IDs, and short non-identifying notes unless consent covers artifact excerpts.
4. Resolve disagreements only after IRR is computed.
