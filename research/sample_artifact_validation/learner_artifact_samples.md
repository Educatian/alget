# ALGET Sample Learner Artifact Set

Purpose: small synthetic validation set for testing whether ALGET's artifact-centered workflow can support human rating, inter-rater reliability, and scorer-human agreement checks before a real pilot.

These samples are not real student data.

## Sample A01: AIL 606 Cognitive Load Diagnosis

Course/section: AIL 606 01.01  
Condition: treatment_annotation_adaptive  
Artifact: cognitive load diagnosis table

Initial artifact: The table lists video, narration, and quiz elements but does not identify learner expertise or distinguish intrinsic from extraneous load.

Annotation evidence: A public connection note links the narration segment to split-attention risk.

AI support decision: The learner accepted a suggestion to add learner audience and segmenting constraint, modified a suggestion that overgeneralized Mayer's modality principle, and rejected a suggestion that removed the source note.

Revised artifact: The table now names novice teachers, separates intrinsic/extraneous/germane load, and adds a limitation that the diagnosis needs usability evidence from a real viewer.

## Sample A02: CAT 531 Teaching Tension Map

Course/section: CAT 531 04.02  
Condition: treatment_annotation_adaptive  
Artifact: Ethobot classroom policy tension map

Initial artifact: The map states that AI should be used responsibly but does not name a classroom value conflict.

Annotation evidence: A confusion note flags uncertainty around privacy and formative feedback.

AI support decision: The learner accepted a prompt to contrast privacy with responsiveness, rejected a suggestion to collect full chat logs, and revised the policy toward minimal necessary data.

Revised artifact: The map identifies privacy versus timely formative support, names teacher review as the boundary condition, and notes that parent-facing use requires a separate consent protocol.

## Sample A03: CAT 100 Resume Evidence Packet

Course/section: CAT 100 02.03  
Condition: comparison_practice_only  
Artifact: resume evidence packet

Initial artifact: The resume includes a broad claim that the learner is an expert in Excel and AI.

Annotation evidence: None.

AI support decision: The learner used a generic wording suggestion without recording what was accepted or rejected.

Revised artifact: The resume wording is cleaner but still overclaims expertise and does not attach a specific spreadsheet artifact.

## Sample A04: AIL 606 Storyboard Revision

Course/section: AIL 606 03.05  
Condition: treatment_annotation_adaptive  
Artifact: storyboard frame sequence

Initial artifact: Frames include narration text and visuals, but the learner action is not visible.

Annotation evidence: An insight note points to signaling and segmenting as design constraints.

AI support decision: The learner accepted a suggestion to add a frame-level learner action, modified the timing recommendation, and rejected a decorative image suggestion that did not support the learning objective.

Revised artifact: The storyboard adds a learner decision point, marks signaling cues, and includes a transfer constraint for advanced learners.
