Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

ALGET (Alabama Generative Intelligent Textbook) supports course reading, practice, tutoring, and learning progress. This policy describes information handled when you use ALGET.

Information we receive

  • Google sign-in: when you choose Google, Google provides basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile image. ALGET does not receive your Google password.
  • Learning activity: ALGET may process your course and section selections, practice answers, submitted text, requests for help, interaction events, session information, and learning progress or mastery estimates.
  • Technical information: the service and its hosting providers may process information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the application, such as browser and request metadata.
  • Local browser storage: some learning progress or preferences may be stored in your browser so the application can continue working when a network service is unavailable.

How information is used

ALGET uses this information to authenticate users, provide course content and learning features, save progress, adapt practice, maintain service security, and evaluate or improve the application. Requests that use AI features are sent to the ALGET application backend for processing; the backend may forward the relevant request to its configured AI provider to return a response.

Service providers and access

ALGET uses Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication and application data, Cloudflare Pages for website delivery, and Cloudflare Workers and Containers for its application backend. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. Course or project staff may access application information when their assigned service permissions allow it. ALGET does not sell personal information.

Retention and requests

Information is retained while needed to provide and maintain ALGET and to meet applicable course, project, or institutional record requirements. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information associated with your account, contact jewoong.moon@gmail.com from the email address used to sign in. Some records may need to be retained where applicable requirements call for it.

Security and your choices

ALGET and its providers use access controls and encrypted connections to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You may stop using Google sign-in at any time; this does not automatically remove information already saved by the service. Do not submit passwords, financial or health information, or other sensitive personal information in learning prompts.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as ALGET changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Questions may be sent to jewoong.moon@gmail.com.
