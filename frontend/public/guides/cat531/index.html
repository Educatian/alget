Narration transcript

Welcome to the CAT 531 ALGET guide. Begin by opening ALGET and choosing Sign in to start. In the Current students box, type your real full name and choose CAT 531. ALGET will open the Technology and Teaching pathway. Start with the diagnostic if it appears, then move through the modules as a sequence of teaching decisions: design tensions, TeachGen@i planning, AI in education, Ethobot ethics, educational technology evaluation, professional vision, field transfer, and instructor toolkit materials when assigned. In each section, read for the decision you need to make, use AI support to test your reasoning, and save evidence that shows how your plan changed because of learner needs, context constraints, equity, policy, or classroom feasibility.

How to use one section well

Name the teaching decision

Before reading deeply, ask: what decision would a teacher need to make here?

Track the constraint

Look for classroom variables such as device access, student variability, family communication, policy, time, and mentor-teacher expectations.

Use AI as a comparison partner

Let AI generate options or critique, but reject suggestions that ignore your learners, invent context, or flatten equity concerns.

Annotate the reason

Leave notes that explain why one option is stronger than another. These notes become evidence of professional judgment.

Produce portfolio evidence

Save the final plan, evaluation brief, transcript annotation, policy note, or field-transfer memo with a short revision explanation.

2. Teaching in Tech-Rich Classrooms With TeachGen@i

Use scenarios to plan routines, adapt for learner variability, and revise based on teacher noticing.

Analyze what AI changes in schools, student work, bias, policy interpretation, and responsible prompting.

Use ethical dialogue, facial-recognition cases, stakeholder analysis, privacy, consent, and transcript annotation.

5. Educational Technology Evaluation

Move from ratings to evidence. Compare vendor claims, usability, accessibility, equity, cost, sustainability, and classroom fit.

Synthesize your modules into a teaching philosophy, implementation plan, risk mitigation note, and presentation of professional vision.

Transfer online cases into field placements through mentor conversations, observation notes, equity audits, and student voice.

8. Instructor Toolkit for CAT 531

Use facilitator notes, troubleshooting, discussion prompts, low-tech fallbacks, and readiness review when assigned by the instructor.

CAT 531 Q&A

Why does CAT 531 start with design tensions instead of tools?

Because teachers need to reason about instructional purpose, learners, equity, and context before selecting or adapting a tool.

Why do I need to type my real name?

Your name lets your instructor connect progress and support signals to the right learner. Do not enter someone else's name or an unrecognizable nickname.

Do I have to follow modules in order?

Use the instructor's pacing first. If no alternate sequence is given, follow the module order because later work depends on design tensions, policy reasoning, and evidence standards introduced earlier.

What counts as strong portfolio evidence?

A strong artifact shows the original idea, the classroom constraint or evidence that changed it, the revision you made, and why that revision is more defensible.

Can I use AI to plan lessons or policy language?

Use AI for brainstorming, critique, adaptation, and comparison. You remain responsible for checking invented context, policy mismatch, equity gaps, and pedagogical fit.

What should I do when TeachGen@i gives a polished but unrealistic suggestion?

Reject or revise it. A polished suggestion that assumes unavailable devices, ignores accommodations, or invents learner details is not classroom-ready.

How should I use Ethobot?

Use Ethobot to practice ethical dialogue, stakeholder analysis, and policy reasoning. The point is not to win the debate; the point is to make tradeoffs visible and defensible.

How is ALGET different from the official course LMS?

ALGET is a learning, practice, and evidence-building environment. Official submission and grading requirements come from the instructor's course materials.

What if I am not in a field placement yet?

Use the field-transfer modules hypothetically, but label the context as simulated or planned. Replace assumptions with real placement evidence as soon as you have it.

What is the best sign that I used a module well?

You can explain a teaching decision with evidence, name a constraint, describe one rejected alternative, and show a revision.
