Narration transcript

Welcome to the CAT 100 ALGET guide. Start by opening ALGET and choosing Sign in to start. In the Current students box, type your real full name and select the correct CAT 100 summer cohort. After you enter, ALGET opens only the CAT 100 pathway. Begin with the diagnostic if it appears. Then work through each module in order: digital citizenship, resume and branding, Excel data stories, teacher conversations with AI, presentations, GitHub Pages, productivity, and final transfer. In every section, read the purpose first, try the embedded checks, use the support tools when you need help, and keep evidence of your work for the assignments your instructor names in the course LMS.

How to use one section well

Read the purpose before the details

Each section begins with why the topic matters. Use that as your target so the page does not feel like random information.

Try the embedded checks honestly

Inline checks, scenarios, and practice prompts are there to reveal gaps. Missing one is useful because the feedback tells you what to fix.

Use BigAL and support actions

Ask for an explanation, a simpler version, practice, or a question. Treat AI help as coaching, not as a substitute for your own work.

Save evidence

For each module, keep the artifact, revision note, or reflection that shows what changed in your thinking or product.

Exit with a next move

Before leaving a section, write one sentence: what you learned, what artifact it affects, and what you will revise next.

CAT 100 Q&A

Which CAT 100 cohort should I choose?

Choose CAT 100 Summer I or CAT 100 Summer II based on the section you are enrolled in. If you choose the wrong one, sign out and enter again with the correct cohort.

Why do I need to type my real name?

Your name lets your instructor connect progress and support signals to the right learner. Do not use nicknames that your instructor cannot identify.

Do I need to finish every section?

Follow your instructor's pacing. The safest default is to move in order because later modules assume earlier habits such as AI verification, revision logs, and evidence collection.

What if I already know Excel, GitHub, or AI tools?

Use the checks quickly and focus on the artifact standard. CAT 100 is not only about tool use; it is about explaining, verifying, and transferring your choices.

Can BigAL or another AI write my assignment for me?

No. Use AI support to understand, brainstorm, critique, or revise. Your final artifact and explanation should show your own decisions and should follow your course AI policy.

Where do I submit assignments?

Use the submission location named by your instructor, usually the official course LMS. ALGET helps you learn and produce evidence, but it may not be the official gradebook.

What should I do if a page feels too long?

Read the learning targets, complete the first check, then use the support rail to ask for a simpler explanation. Return to the worked example before trying the final artifact task.

What if audio, video, or an interaction does not load?

Refresh once, try a current Chrome or Edge browser, and check your network. If it still fails, continue with the transcript or text and report the exact section to your instructor.

How is my progress used?

Progress and support signals help the instructor see where students may need help. Avoid entering another student's name because it can misattribute learning records.

What is the best sign that I used a module well?

You can point to an artifact, a revision, and a short explanation of why the change made the work stronger.
