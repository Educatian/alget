# Course Heading Style Guide

This repository uses **Title Case** for course headings, subheadings, callout titles, panel labels, quiz labels, and diagram titles.

## Scope

- Applies to `##`, `###`, and `####` headings in course content.
- Applies to in-body callout titles such as `Reflect`, `The Big Idea`, and `Check Your Understanding`.
- Applies to UI-facing panel titles and labels such as `Knowledge Check`, `Practice`, `Learning Objectives`, and `Interactive Simulation`.
- Applies to diagram and illustration titles such as `Concept Illustration` and `Technical Illustration`.
- Applies across all courses unless a domain-specific notation requires an exception.

## Rules

1. Use **Title Case**, not sentence case.
2. Capitalize the first and last word.
3. Capitalize major words:
   - nouns
   - pronouns
   - verbs
   - adjectives
   - adverbs
   - subordinating conjunctions
4. Lowercase minor words unless they are the first or last word:
   - `a`, `an`, `and`, `as`, `at`, `but`, `by`, `for`, `from`, `in`, `into`, `of`, `on`, `or`, `the`, `to`, `with`
5. After a colon, capitalize the first word of the next phrase.
6. Preserve standard technical formatting:
   - `AI`, `XR`, `CTML`, `ZPD`, `ADDIE`, `ABCD`, `WCAG`, `UDL`
   - `CoP`
   - `van der Waals`
   - `vs.`
7. Capitalize both sides of meaningful hyphenated compounds:
   - `Real-World`
   - `Self-Healing`
   - `Bio-Inspired`
   - `Non-Conservative`
8. Preserve possessives naturally:
   - `Gecko's`
   - `Mayer's`
   - `Keller's`
   - `Hooke's`
9. Keep sentence-style prompts and helper text in natural sentence case:
   - `How confident are you in this answer?`
   - `Write a concise explanation in your own words.`
   - `We could not generate the assessment right now.`
10. For compact buttons, chips, and panel headings, prefer concise Title Case:
   - `Generate Quiz`
   - `Review Missed Concepts`
   - `Save for Later`
   - `Live Presence On`

## Preferred Examples

- `What Is Instructional Design?`
- `Design Heuristics for Constructivist Learning`
- `The Physics of Micro-Serrations`
- `Bio-Inspired Link: Nature's Trusses`
- `Step A: Write the Initial and Final Energies`
- `Reflect:`
- `Check Your Understanding`
- `Interactive Simulation`
- `Technical Illustration`
- `Live Presence On`

## Avoid

- `What is instructional design?`
- `The physics of micro-serrations`
- `Bio-inspired link: nature's trusses`
- `Step A: write the initial and final energies`
- `Generate quiz`
- `Live presence on`
- `Technical illustration`
