    ## Transfer Move

    **CAT 531 context.** Mentor Teacher Conversation Guides belongs to **Field-Based Transfer and Coaching**, where learners work on teacher technology judgment and AI ethics. The section uses a concrete artifact instead of a generic reflection prompt: **school AI policy memo with privacy, equity, consent, and learning-purpose checks**.

    The section is designed so the learner can move the same judgment pattern to a new tool, audience, or classroom setting. Transfer is visible only when the learner names the constraint that changes across contexts.

    The learner's first task is to decide what the artifact claims. The claim must name an audience, a constraint, and a reason the artifact matters. For this section, the relevant constraints come from **TPACK**, **critical edtech studies**, and **AI and education datafication**. A polished artifact that does not expose those constraints is not yet high-quality intelligent textbook evidence.

    ## Adaptive Support Rule

    ALGET should use the following decision rule for this section:

    - If the annotation is a **question**, offer a focused explanation tied to **TPACK**.
    - If the annotation is **confusion**, offer a worked contrast between a weak and strong school AI policy memo with privacy, equity, consent, and learning-purpose checks.
    - If the annotation is an **insight**, ask the learner to transfer the decision to a new audience.
    - If the annotation is a **connection**, ask for evidence that the connection changed the artifact.
